Introducing **PinePods 0.9.1** – and don't let the small version bump fool you. This release rebuilds podcast sync from the ground up, refreshes every feed on its own schedule, shares one copy of each feed between users, reworks hosts and guests, adds an optional AI container for transcripts and ad skipping, rebuilds the mobile player and its look, brings YouTube channels to mobile, and lets your devices control each other. Firewood, the terminal client, gets its 0.2.0 release alongside it. It also fixes eight security issues, so please upgrade soon.

> **Before you upgrade:**
> * **Back up first.** The upgrade merges every user's copy of each episode into one shared set. It can't be undone, and backups taken before the upgrade can't be restored afterwards. There’s no reason that the upgrade would fail, but you can’t return to a previous version.
> * **Sync apps (AntennaPod etc.) must sign in with an app password.** Create one in Settings → Podcast Sync and enter it in each app once.
> * **Port 8042 is gone.** If a sync app or reverse proxy pointed at `:8042`, point it at your Pinepods URL instead. This was the gpodder api port, previously it was routed through nginx which still happens today, so unless you were specfically forwarding 8042 (and you shouldn't have been) don't worry about this.
> * **`PINEPODS_REFRESH_CRON` is deprecated** → use `PINEPODS_REFRESH_MAX_INTERVAL` (default `24h`).
> * **The first startup takes longer than usual** on big libraries while migrations merge duplicates and every feed is read in full once.
>
> The [release notes](https://github.com/madeofpendletonwool/PinePods/releases/tag/0.9.1) have the full upgrade section.

## Security

* **Eight security fixes** – All affect 0.9.0 and earlier. The advisories are linked from the [release notes](https://github.com/madeofpendletonwool/PinePods/releases/tag/0.9.1). If you can't upgrade right away, turn off OIDC login and only give accounts to people you trust.
* **Ownership checks across the API** – A new test suite checks that every per-user endpoint refuses other users' data.
* **Safer by default** – Credentials are redacted from logs, stored feed passwords are encrypted, a podcast's RSS link only exposes that podcast, and Gravatar is off by default going forward.

---

## Podcast Sync, Rebuilt

* **The gpodder server is now part of the Pinepods API** – One less service in the container, and changes from your apps reach Pinepods the moment they sync.
* **App passwords** – Create a separate password for each app and revoke it on its own. Your account password and MFA stay out of third-party apps.
* **External gpodder and Nextcloud sync, rebuilt** – Nothing is lost when a sync fails, positions no longer reset to 0 in AntennaPod, and "both" (Pinepods server plus an external one) actually works.
* **New sync settings** – One place for the sync server, app passwords, devices, external servers and sync status.
* **No more timeouts on big libraries** – And MariaDB/MySQL installs can sync again.

---

## Every Feed on Its Own Schedule

* **Adaptive refresh** – Busy shows are checked about every 15 minutes, weekly shows every few hours and quiet ones once a day.
* **Podping (optional)** – Many podcast hosts announce new episodes as they publish. Turn on Podping and Pinepods picks them up within about a minute, matching announcements locally so what you follow never leaves your server.
* **Lighter on podcast hosts** – Compressed downloads, unchanged feeds skipped, and busy hosts left alone for as long as they ask.
* **Feed problems you can see** – Settings → Feed problems lists podcasts whose refresh keeps failing, and you get a message when a feed breaks and another when it recovers.

---

## Shared Feeds and Episodes

* **One copy per podcast** – Users subscribed to the same show now share one copy of its episodes. Adding a show someone else already follows is quicker, and the database no longer grows with every user.
* **No more duplicates** – Feeds known under another address are matched to the existing podcast, and existing duplicate episodes and podcasts are merged on upgrade, keeping your furthest listen position.
* **Podcast details follow the feed** – Titles, descriptions and artwork update when the feed changes, unless you've edited them yourself.

---

## Hosts and Guests, Rebuilt

* **Stored at refresh** – Hosts and guests come from Podcasting 2.0 tags and PodPeopleDB and show up with the page instead of loading separately.
* **One identity per person** – Following someone follows them on every show they appear on.
* **Everywhere** – Person pages, People You Follow and adding people, on both web and mobile.

---

## AI Features (Optional)

* **New `pinepods-ai` container** – A separate, fully optional service for the heavy ML work, published for amd64 and arm64.
* **Transcripts** – Generate a transcript for any episode, viewable on web and in the new mobile player.
* **Ad detection and skip** _(beta)_ – Ads are detected server-side and skipped on web and mobile, built to handle dynamically inserted ads.
* **Trim silence** – Skip silent gaps during playback.

---

## Control Playback Across Your Devices

* **See what's playing on your other devices** – Web tabs, phones and desktop apps – and control them: play/pause, seek, skip, speed.
* **Play here / Send here** – Pick up an episode at another device's live position, or hand this device's playback to another one.
* **A smarter queue** – Adding an episode puts it right after what's playing, and finished episodes leave the queue. YouTube videos are first-class queue items.

## Firewood 0.2.0: Pinepods in Your Terminal

Firewood, the terminal client for Pinepods, gets its first release since 0.1.0 alongside 0.9.1 – and it's rebuilt from the ground up.

* **A proper terminal app** – A sidebar with every page one key away, a command palette (`:` or Ctrl+P), mouse support, rebindable keys and the same 25 themes as the web app.
* **Streaming that just works** – Starts in a second or two, seeks anywhere, changes speed without changing pitch, skips detected ads and silence, and saves your progress to the server as you listen.
* **Queue, playlists, chapters and transcripts** – Plus cover art right in the terminal on kitty, sixel and iTerm2.
* **Part of your devices** – Firewood shows up in the web and mobile apps' device lists, so you can beam an episode to it from your phone, and control your other devices from it.
* **Headless mode** – `pinepods_firewood --headless` turns a Raspberry Pi or spare machine into a speaker your other Pinepods apps play on.
* **Media keys** – Your keyboard's play/pause and skip keys work, and what's playing shows in the system's media controls on macOS and Linux.

Downloads for Linux, macOS and Windows are on the [Firewood releases page](https://github.com/madeofpendletonwool/pinepods-firewood/releases/tag/0.2.0).

## A New Mobile App

* **Rebuilt player** – Faster stream start, pause and seek, a new Now Playing screen with chapters, transcript and Up Next, and playback speed that finally sticks.
* **Fresh look** – A new soft UI with Phosphor icons and IBM Plex Sans, redesigned podcast and episode pages, and a mini player that stays put.
* **Finished means finished** – Episodes are marked finished on your server when they end, and auto-play next follows your playlists, auto-play-next podcasts and queue.
* **Download management** – Wi-Fi-only downloads, automatic queue downloads, cleanup after finishing or after N days, and reuse of copies your server already downloaded.
* **Android Auto and CarPlay** – Seek and skip buttons fixed, with a configurable skip interval.
* **Custom TLS certificates** – Connect to servers using your own CA or client certificates.

---

## YouTube Channels

* **On mobile** – Search, preview and subscribe to YouTube channels right from the app.
* **Faster refresh** – Pinepods checks each channel's feed and only runs yt-dlp for new videos, once for everyone who follows it.
* **Video filters** – Only keep videos over or under a length (goodbye shorts), or with certain words in the title.
* **yt-dlp updates itself** – So YouTube breakage no longer waits for a Pinepods release.

---

## Internationalization

* **Translations caught up** – Danish, French and Spanish updates from Weblate plus translators' pending suggestions. Anything untranslated falls back to English.
* **Want to help?** – Head over to [Weblate](https://hosted.weblate.org/projects/pinepods) and fill in any translations you can. The work there never ends.

## Fixes, Polish & Under the Hood

* **Downloads** – Files over 2 GiB, signed CDN links, SD cards on Android 13+, and removing a podcast now removes its downloaded files.
* **MariaDB/MySQL** – Playlist errors and duplicate history entries fixed.
* **Startup** – Pinepods waits for the database to come up and explains clearly when Valkey/Redis can't be reached.
* **Download metadata** – Downloaded files get proper ID3 tags, with optional cover art and metadata sidecars.
* **Tests** – A new integration test suite runs against PostgreSQL and MariaDB on every change.
* Countless smaller fixes across web and mobile.
